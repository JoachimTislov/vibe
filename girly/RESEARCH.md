# Girly Platform - User Needs Research

## Overview

This document contains comprehensive research on what girls and women need from a digital platform, organized by feature area. The goal is to create a platform that authentically serves the diverse needs, interests, and emotional experiences of its users.

> **Core Philosophy**: Technology should adapt to human emotions and experiences, not the other way around. Girls and women experience a full spectrum of emotions - joy, sadness, excitement, anxiety, love, anger - and our platform should support all of these authentically.

---

## 1. Virtual Pet Companion

### Core Concept
A digital pet that provides emotional support, companionship, and reacts to the user's mood and activities across the platform.

### Why Girls Need This
- **Emotional Support**: Pets provide unconditional love and acceptance, which is especially valuable during difficult times
- **Responsibility Training**: Caring for a pet teaches responsibility and routine
- **Loneliness Reduction**: A virtual companion can alleviate feelings of loneliness
- **Safe Emotional Outlet**: Users can express emotions to their pet without fear of judgment
- **Achievement Motivation**: Pet growth and happiness provide positive reinforcement

### Features

#### Pet Types
| Type | Personality | Special Abilities | Target Age Group |
|------|-------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Cat** | Independent, affectionate | Can be left alone, purrs when happy | 13+ |
| **Dog** | Loyal, energetic | Needs walks, very social | 10+ |
| **Unicorn** | Magical, wise | Can fly, grants wishes | 8-14 |
| **Dragon** | Protective, fierce | Breathes fire, guards treasures | 12+ |
| **Rabbit** | Gentle, curious | Fast learner, loves carrots | 8+ |
| **Fox** | Clever, playful | Can solve puzzles with user | 10+ |
| **Custom Creature** | User-designed | Unique abilities based on design | All ages |

#### Pet Customization
- **Appearance**: Coat color, patterns, accessories, size
- **Name**: User-chosen name with personality matching
- **Personality**: Select from predefined or create custom traits
- **Backstory**: Create a story for how you found your pet

#### Pet Interactions

##### Daily Care
- **Feeding**: Different foods affect mood and energy
  - Healthy food: +health, +mood
  - Junk food: +happiness, -health
  - Special treats: +affection, +energy
- **Playing**: Mini-games with your pet
  - Fetch
  - Hide and seek
  - Puzzle solving
  - Dance battles
- **Grooming**: Brushing, bathing, nail trimming
- **Training**: Teach tricks and commands

##### Emotional Bonding
- **Talk to Pet**: Type messages to your pet and get responses
- **Pet Reactions**: Pet reacts to user's mood (from mood-service integration)
  - When user is sad: Pet snuggles close, offers comfort
  - When user is happy: Pet celebrates, plays, shows excitement
  - When user is anxious: Pet stays close, offers calming presence
- **Pet Mood**: Pet has its own mood that changes based on care and user interaction
- **Pet Journal**: Pet writes entries about its day with the user

##### Growth & Evolution
- **Leveling System**: Pet gains XP from interactions and levels up
- **Evolution**: Pet can evolve into new forms at certain levels
- **Skills Tree**: Unlock new abilities and traits
- **Achievements**: Earn badges for milestones (1 day together, 1 week, etc.)

##### Social Features
- **Pet Visits**: Friends' pets can visit and interact
- **Pet Dates**: Arrange playdates between pets
- **Pet Competitions**: Beauty contests, talent shows, races
- **Pet Gifting**: Send gifts to friends' pets
- **Pet Marriage**: Two pets can "marry" and have babies

##### Cross-Service Integration
- **Mood Integration**: Pet reacts to user's logged mood
- **Wardrobe Integration**: Dress up your pet with outfits from wardrobe
- **Makeup Integration**: Apply accessories to your pet
- **Drama Integration**: Pet can participate in drama scenarios
- **Social Integration**: Pet can join social games and activities

#### Monetization (Future)
- **Premium Pets**: Special rare pets for subscribers
- **Pet Accessories**: Cosmetic items that don't affect gameplay
- **Pet Food**: Special foods with unique effects
- **Pet Houses**: Customizable pet living spaces

#### Emotional Support Features
- **Comfort Mode**: Pet provides extra support when user is having a bad day
- **Motivation**: Pet encourages user to complete tasks and goals
- **Therapy Mode**: Guided interactions for emotional processing
- **Memory Book**: Pet helps user remember positive moments

## 2. Wardrobe & Fashion

### Core Concept
A comprehensive virtual closet and fashion assistant that helps users explore style, organize clothing, and express themselves through fashion.

### Why Girls Need This
- **Self-Expression**: Clothing is a primary way to express identity and mood
- **Organization**: Helps keep track of wardrobe and reduce decision fatigue
- **Creative Outlet**: Experiment with different looks without financial commitment
- **Body Positivity**: Explore styles that make user feel confident
- **Practical Tool**: Reduces morning stress by planning outfits in advance
- **Social Connection**: Share style with friends, get feedback

### Features

#### Virtual Closet
- **Add Items**: Upload photos or select from catalog
  - Photo upload with automatic color detection
  - Categorize by type (tops, bottoms, dresses, etc.)
  - Tag with brands, colors, occasions
- **Item Details**:
  - Purchase date and price
  - Last worn date
  - Condition (new, good, worn)
  - Seasonality
  - Care instructions
- **Smart Organization**:
  - Automatic categorization using AI
  - Search by color, type, brand, occasion
  - Filter by season, weather, etc.

#### Outfit Planning
- **Outfit Builder**: Drag-and-drop interface to create outfits
- **Outfit Calendar**: Plan outfits for the week/month
- **Weather Integration**: Suggest outfits based on weather forecast
- **Event Suggestions**: Outfit ideas for specific events (party, work, date, etc.)
- **Color Coordination**: AI-powered color matching suggestions

#### Style Exploration
- **Virtual Try-On**: Upload selfie and virtually try on clothes
  - AR-powered overlay (mobile app feature)
  - Size estimation based on user measurements
- **Style Quiz**: Discover your personal style
- **Trend Forecast**: See upcoming trends and how to incorporate them
- **Body Type Guide**: Find styles that flatter your body type
- **Color Analysis**: Discover which colors suit you best

#### Social Features
- **Outfit Sharing**: Post outfits to feed or share with friends
- **Style Feedback**: Friends can like, comment, and rate outfits
- **Outfit Challenges**: Weekly themes (e.g., "90s nostalgia", "business casual")
- **Style Swap**: Trade virtual clothing items with friends
- **Inspiration Boards**: Create mood boards for different styles
- **Follow Influencers**: Follow platform creators and see their outfits

#### Shopping Integration
- **Wishlist**: Save items you want to buy
- **Price Tracking**: Monitor prices of wished items
- **Similar Items**: Find similar items at different price points
- **Affiliate Links**: Earn commission on purchases made through platform
- **Sustainable Fashion**: Filter for eco-friendly, thrifting, etc.

#### Smart Features
- **Wardrobe Analytics**:
  - Most worn items
  - Least worn items (suggest wearing them)
  - Color distribution
  - Brand distribution
- **Cost Per Wear**: Calculate value of each item
- **Missing Pieces**: Identify gaps in wardrobe
- **Capsule Wardrobe**: Create minimalist, versatile wardrobes

#### Cross-Service Integration
- **Pet Outfits**: Dress up your virtual pet
- **Makeup Matching**: Coordinate makeup with outfits
- **Drama Scenarios**: Outfits for drama story characters
- **Mood-Based Suggestions**: Outfit suggestions based on mood
- **Social Games**: Fashion-based mini-games

## 4. Drama Simulation

### Core Concept
A life simulation game that allows users to create, experience, and resolve dramatic situations in a safe, virtual environment.

### Why Girls Need This
- **Emotional Processing**: Safely explore and resolve real-life emotions
- **Decision Practice**: Practice making tough decisions without real-world consequences
- **Creativity**: Create compelling stories and characters
- **Community**: Share stories and get feedback
- **Entertainment**: Fun, engaging gameplay
- **Self-Reflection**: Understand personal values and boundaries
- **Empathy Building**: Experience different perspectives

### Features

#### Story Modes

##### Pre-Made Stories
- **Relationship Drama**: Navigate friendships, crushes, breakups
- **Family Drama**: Deal with parents, siblings, extended family
- **School/Work Drama**: Handle bullies, office politics, competition
- **Life Decisions**: Career choices, moves, major life changes
- **Fantasy Drama**: Supernatural elements, magic, alternate realities
- **Historical Drama**: Stories set in different time periods

##### Custom Stories
- **Story Creator**: Build your own drama scenarios
- **Character Customization**: Create unique characters with backgrounds, personalities
- **Branching Paths**: Create multiple story branches based on choices
- **Choice Consequences**: Define how choices affect the story
- **Multiple Endings**: Create different outcomes based on user decisions

#### Gameplay Mechanics
- **Choice-Based**: Make decisions that affect the story outcome
- **Stat System**: Track character attributes (happiness, anger, stress, etc.)
- **Relationship Meter**: Track relationship strength with other characters
- **Inventory**: Collect items that affect gameplay
- **Skills**: Develop character skills over time
- **Time System**: Day/night cycles, seasons, aging

#### Character Creation
- **Appearance**: Customize hair, eyes, clothing, body type
- **Personality**: Select traits (kind, selfish, brave, etc.)
- **Backstory**: Create character history and motivations
- **Relationships**: Define pre-existing relationships with other characters
- **Secrets**: Give characters hidden motives or knowledge

#### Emotional Depth
- **Emotion Wheel**: Characters experience complex, layered emotions
- **Mental Health**: Storylines address anxiety, depression, trauma
- **Real Consequences**: Actions have realistic emotional impacts
- **Support Systems**: Characters can access therapy, friends, family for help
- **Trigger Warnings**: Clear warnings for sensitive content

#### Multiplayer Mode
- **Collaborative Stories**: Create stories together with friends
- **Role-Playing**: Take on character roles in group stories
- **Vote on Decisions**: Let the group decide story direction
- **Competitive Drama**: Compete to create the most popular story
- **Story Contests**: Weekly themes with voting and prizes

#### Social Features
- **Story Sharing**: Publish stories for others to play
- **Rating & Reviews**: Rate and review community stories
- **Favorites**: Save favorite stories to play again
- **Comments**: Discuss stories with other players
- **Fan Fiction**: Create spin-offs of popular stories
- **Story Recommendations**: Get suggestions based on preferences

#### Cross-Service Integration
- **User Avatars**: Use your wardrobe outfits for story characters
- **Pet Characters**: Include your virtual pet in stories
- **Makeup Looks**: Apply makeup looks to characters
- **Mood Tracking**: Story events can affect user's logged mood
- **Social Integration**: Invite friends to play stories together

## Age-Appropriate Content

Different features and content are appropriate for different age groups. The platform should implement age-appropriate filtering and parental controls.

### Age Groups

#### Ages 8-12 (Pre-Teen)
- **Focus**: Fun, creativity, learning, socialization
- **Appropriate Features**:
  - Virtual pets (all types)
  - Simple mini-games
  - Basic wardrobe organization
  - Fun makeup try-on (no permanent changes)
  - Simple drama stories (no mature themes)
  - Basic mood tracking (emoji-based)
  - Parent-controlled social features
- **Restricted Features**:
  - Advanced social features
  - Mature drama scenarios
  - Detailed journaling
  - Resource directories

#### Ages 13-17 (Teen)
- **Focus**: Identity exploration, social connection, skill development
- **Appropriate Features**:
  - All pre-teen features
  - Advanced social features (with privacy controls)
  - Full makeup tutorials
  - Complex wardrobe planning
  - Detailed mood tracking and journaling
  - Moderate drama scenarios
  - Resource directories (age-appropriate)
  - Community support groups
- **Restricted Features**:
  - Mature themes in drama
  - Some resource directories (e.g., domestic violence)

#### Ages 18+ (Adult)
- **Focus**: Full platform access, self-care, community
- **Appropriate Features**:
  - All teen features
  - Full drama scenarios (including mature themes)
  - Complete resource directories
  - All social features
  - Advanced analytics
  - Monetization features

### Parental Controls
- **Content Filtering**: Filter by age-appropriateness
- **Usage Limits**: Set time limits for platform usage
- **Privacy Controls**: Control who can interact with child's account
- **Activity Monitoring**: View child's activity (with transparency)
- **Approval Settings**: Require approval for certain actions
- **Safe Mode**: Additional protections for younger users

## Accessibility Considerations

The platform should be accessible to users with various abilities and needs:

### Visual Accessibility
- **Color Contrast**: High contrast mode, colorblind-friendly palette
- **Text Size**: Adjustable font sizes
- **Screen Reader**: Full screen reader compatibility
- **Alt Text**: Descriptions for all images
- **Dark Mode**: Reduce eye strain
- **Reduced Motion**: For users with vestibular disorders

### Auditory Accessibility
- **Captions**: For all video content
- **Transcripts**: For audio content
- **Visual Alternatives**: For sound-based features
- **Volume Controls**: Independent volume for different features

### Motor Accessibility
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Full keyboard support
- **Voice Control**: Voice commands for key actions
- **Large Touch Targets**: For mobile users
- **Customizable Controls**: Remap controls as needed
- **Timeout Adjustments**: Extend time limits for actions

### Cognitive Accessibility
- **Simple Language**: Option for simplified text
- **Clear Navigation**: Consistent, predictable interface
- **Focus Mode**: Reduce distractions
- **Read Aloud**: Text-to-speech for content
- **Break Reminders**: Suggest taking breaks
- **Step-by-Step Guides**: For complex tasks

### Emotional Accessibility
- **Trigger Warnings**: Clear warnings for sensitive content
- **Content Filtering**: Filter out triggering topics
- **Safe Mode**: Additional protections during vulnerable times
- **Emergency Exit**: Quick exit to neutral screen
- **Mental Health Days**: Platform can suggest taking a break

## Conclusion

This research document provides a comprehensive foundation for building a platform that authentically serves the diverse needs of girls and women. The key insights are:

1. **Emotional Authenticity**: The platform must support the full spectrum of human emotions, not just the "positive" ones.

2. **Diverse Needs**: Girls and women have varied interests - from practical organization to creative expression to emotional support.

3. **Social Connection**: Human connection is a fundamental need that the platform should facilitate in safe, meaningful ways.

4. **Self-Expression**: The platform should provide multiple avenues for users to express their identity and creativity.

5. **Safety and Privacy**: User safety, privacy, and well-being must be the top priorities in all design decisions.

6. **Inclusivity**: The platform should be welcoming and accessible to all users, regardless of age, ability, culture, or identity.

7. **Authenticity**: The platform should feel genuine and true to the experiences of its users, not a sanitized or commercialized version of femininity.

By building with these principles in mind, the Girly Platform can become a truly valuable and supportive digital companion for its users.
